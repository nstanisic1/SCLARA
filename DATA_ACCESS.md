# Data access and attribution

Public distribution: [GitHub](https://github.com/nstanisic1/SCLARA). Software version 2026.11.09.4: [Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24088352](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24088352). This DOI identifies the public code and permitted-data release; the complete local computational archive has the separate access conditions below.

The code, examples, tests and accompanying software documentation are released under the MIT licence. This licence does not apply to third-party data or to the manuscript and its figures. Aggregate posterior contrast tables and numerical diagnostics produced for this study may be reused with attribution to Nemanja Stanisic and SCLARA. Cite the original data providers as well when reusing their evidence.

`SOURCES.tsv` is the file-level citation catalogue (Online Resource 3). It records the source, role, version or access date, acquisition URL, retained locator, checksum and source-specific access conditions. The manuscript and supplement cite the principal datasets and original reports where they are used. Publisher supplementary tables remain attributed to their original articles; standalone datasets have their own persistent identifiers.

The public distribution contains the analysis code, frozen specifications, synthetic example, portable tests, aggregate posterior contrast tables and numerical diagnostics, source catalogue, public archaeological-coordinate inventory, and manuscript and supplement. It excludes third-party source snapshots, reconciled paternal records, participant-linked cohorts and locality inventories, and archived Git history from the full research workspace. Online Resource 2 contains archaeological site and geometry representatives and published ancient find locations; it excludes the 1,404 participant-linked paternal-origin positions. The separate full coordinate inventory is confidential reviewer material, not an ordinary online supplement.

The synthetic example and Python contract tests run without empirical data. Full Swedish reconstruction requires the exact authorised inputs described in the source catalogue, including the original paternal-source snapshots and native archaeological records. Obtain public inputs from their original providers and respect their terms. Current downloads may differ from the frozen inputs; compare checksums before treating them as an exact reconstruction.

The complete local reproducibility archive includes frozen inputs, all retained coefficient draws and conditional result families, acquisition records, and reconstruction instructions. For research verification or editorial review, contact Nemanja Stanisic at nstanisic@singidunum.ac.rs. Access to restricted components is subject to the original providers' conditions and appropriate protection of personal information; this contact route is not a blanket permission to redistribute them.

For a verification request, identify archive version 2026.11.09.4 and the requested inputs or analyses. The corresponding author will check source permissions before arranging confidential access. Reviewers may make the request through the handling editor. Approved transfers should use a private journal facility or a recipient-restricted link; the archive and participant-linked inventory must not be posted as public supplementary material. Where a provider does not permit transfer, obtain that component directly from the provider and compare its checksum with the source catalogue. Receipt of an archive does not confer permission for further redistribution.
